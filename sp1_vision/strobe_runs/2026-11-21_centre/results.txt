2026-11-21 ball centre by cone fit - results
Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-ball-centre-cone-fit-design.md
Code: sp1_vision/sphere_fit.py; orientation_check --centre circle|cone (default cone).

1. Synthetic (diagnosis.txt, centre_diagnosis.py)
   Oversampled renders with cam1's K and distortion. The circle fits
   (refine_ball, refine_ball_by_contrast) put the centre radially OUTWARD:
   +0.4 px at 10 deg off axis, +0.8..1.0 px at 20 deg, ~1.5 px at 30 deg.
   That error comes from the projection, not from pixel noise: the outline
   of an off-axis sphere is an ellipse whose centre lies outward, and the
   lens distortion adds to it. JPEG q85 and noise sigma 2 hardly change it.
   The cone fit: median 0.02-0.12 px everywhere, no radial trend. At 1.7 deg
   per px that removes ~1.5 deg of orientation at 20 deg off axis.

2. Two corrections the real frames forced (both pinned in test_sphere_fit)
   - The outline rule. frame_analysis._outermost_edge_per_ray takes the
     outermost edge that reaches 3/4 of the strongest edge on its ray. On the
     real ball the lower limb is dim (the IR comes from above), so a mark's
     edge out-crisps the limb. The first version took mark edges 13-15 px
     inside and put the static pair's centre 3.8 px off. Now the search runs
     in a narrow band round the circle fit, takes only edges that get darker
     outward, and sets its threshold from the limb over all rays. Later
     passes search along the outline the cone predicts: off axis that is an
     ellipse, not a circle round the projected axis.
   - cv2.remap rounds sample positions to 1/32 px on OpenCV 4.5.4. That gave
     0.13 px on the Jetson against 0.04 px on Windows with identical images.
     sphere_fit now samples with its own exact bilinear interpolation.

   The three frames the cone fit no longer tracks (putt 2: 16.3913, 16.4109;
   putt 3: 45.1117) are monocular end frames. Their circle centres were
   4.2 / 9.0 / 5.2 mm off across the ray and ~20 mm along it, so they were
   wrong anyway; no usable frame was lost. More frames are now found in
   stereo (monocular 26 -> 16). The pair gates were tuned on circle radii,
   and the cone's correct radius passes them as well.

   Against the plan's yardstick:
   - across-ray residual better in all three putts
     (median 1.21 / 0.66 / 1.29 -> 0.81 / 0.50 / 0.57 mm);
   - axis error better in all three (14.6 / 12.8 / 6.9 -> 7.6 / 4.5 / 5.5 deg);
   - every sign right (60 of 60), no usable frame lost.
   The depth scatter along the ray falls as well (3.4-5.9 -> 0.9-4.5 mm), so
   the cone centre agrees better between the two cameras.

   Not better everywhere:
   - putt 3 has fewer cam1 Orientations (11 -> 9), and its two both-camera
     frames agree worse (3.55 / 5.40 deg, n = 2);
   - on the static pair cam1 now refuses: its cross at ~57 deg was already
     borderline and reads as a dot after the ~1 px centre shift. cam2 still
     orients, and no wrong pose is returned.

4. Decision
   The cone centre is orientation_check's default (CENTRE = "cone");
   --centre circle reproduces the 2026-11-21_orientation tables. The static
   acceptance number (2.60 deg) stays recorded with the circle centre:
   test_static_pair_both_cameras_agree runs with CENTRE = "circle".

   Still open: in a typical frame the centre is now good to ~0.5 mm across
   the ray, but 8-13 frames per putt are still flagged (> 0.8 mm). The
   commonest refusal, and so the next limit, is now the classification of
   marks near the limb (crosses read as dots).

   Not changed: ball_pair's own default refiner, setup_check and
   strobe_event still use the circle centre (spec non-goal). The setup
   check's one-pixel depth steps (strobe_runs README, 2026-11-18) make it a
   candidate.

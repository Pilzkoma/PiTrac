2. Rolling putts (triggered: 0.5 ms exposure, 100 us flash every 10 ms)
   How the ball is found. find_ball_pair alone fails here: the putts are
   pushed with a slipper that stays next to the ball, and the plain search
   says "ambiguous" in most frames (putt 1: 26 of 30) and in a few gives a
   wrong unambiguous answer (Z 278 / 327 mm on the foot). So the roll follows
   a track seeded by the marks: a stereo position whose image gives an
   Orientation in BOTH cameras agreeing within 3 deg is the ball; from there
   each next frame is searched only within 25 mm of the position predicted
   by a line through the 5 nearest tracked positions. Where stereo fails
   (the ball reaches cam2's right edge, ~0.15-0.25 s into each putt) the
   centre comes from one camera: the circle centre's ray met with the plane
   the stereo track rolled in ("m" frames in the tables).
   Frame times: arrival times come in USB pairs 8/12 ms apart while trigger
   edges are exactly 10 ms apart; steps are snapped to whole 10 ms.
   Rolling model: angle |v| dt / r about up x v_hat, up from the 2026-11-11
   attitude (pitch +1.60 deg), v from fit_motion over +-60 ms of track.

   Centre quality (fix round 1, review of task 5). The tracker can hand
   orient_image a wrong centre; nothing flagged it. How much a centre error
   costs was measured directly (cam1 16.1389 and 16.1509 s, both 5-mark
   Orientations, centre moved and the frame re-oriented):
       along camera 1's ray   +-0.5..2 mm: 0.0-0.2 deg;  10 mm: 0.8 deg
       across the ray         +-0.5 mm: 1.9-2.0 deg;  1 mm: 3.7-4.5 deg;
                              2 mm: 7.4-8.2 deg   (about 1/r per mm, 2.7
                              deg, plus more for marks off the view centre)
   So stereo depth scatter (along the ray, residual median 3-6 mm, up to
   21 mm) hardly matters, and a centre error across the ray matters a lot.
   Each tracked frame now gets the residual of its centre from a parabola
   fitted through the other tracked centres within +-60 ms, split along /
   across the ray (columns "along across" in the tables), and is flagged
   when the across part exceeds 0.8 mm - the size that alone costs the
   static acceptance number of 3 deg (3 / 3.75 deg per mm) - or when a
   circle used for the centre comes within 2 px of the image edge. On the
   cleanly tracked middle of putt 2 (16.11-16.30 s) the across residual is
   0.2-0.6 mm in most frames, so 0.8 mm is also about twice the residual of
   a good stretch. Flagged frames: 15 of 25 (putt 1), 19 of 38 (putt 2: 18
   across, 2 at cam2's edge), 17 of 25 (putt 3). Median across residual
   1.21 / 0.66 / 1.29 mm - typical centres are NOT good to 3 deg.

   What the numbers support: where the ball centre is good to 0.8 mm across
   the line of sight, the rolling check meets the plan's expectation (13
   comparisons: axis 5 deg median, angle 3 %, every sign right). The axis
   failures of putts 1 and 2 come from the ball centre - the circle centre
   in the image and the tracker that keeps frames whose centre is a few
   pixels off - not from the mark identification. But only 13 of 49
   comparisons have such a centre: on these frames the centre, not the
   marks, limits the precision, and 1 px of circle-centre error (0.45 mm
   at 420 mm) already costs ~1.7 deg. The method needs a ball centre good
   to well under a pixel across the line of sight before its axis is
   within 10 deg routinely; 13 comparisons are too few to call the
   expectation met.
   Physics in the early part: right after the push the ball turns slower
   than rolling and catches up - putt 3 -56, -41, -30, -14, -7, +3 %, putt 2
   -22, -19, -15, -5, -1 % - the skid the plan allowed for, with the right
   sign throughout.

3. What limits the share of frames with an Orientation
   a) Real marks are smaller than the map's ink areas. Matched marks over
      the three putts: crosses median 38.1 mm2 (map ink 57.1, ratio 0.67,
      smallest 34.5 - just above the band's lower edge 0.6 x 57.1 = 34.3),
      bars 30.8 mm2 (ink 38.1, ratio 0.81), dots 27.1 mm2 (ink 37.8, 0.72).
      73 cross-like blobs (round, not solid) of 20-34 mm2 fell below the band
      and became clutter. So crosses are cut off by AREA_BAND; dots and bars
      are inside it. (The static frame shows the same: cam1's cross at 57 deg,
      31.8 mm2, unclassified.) Probable causes: the 0.6 x brightness threshold
      takes only the core of a soft (MJPEG, focus) edge, and the 2 mm opening
      rounds the arm tips - a cross has the most edge per area.
   b) Crosses near the limb read as dots: blurred arms fill the notches,
      solidity 0.85-0.86 against SOLID 0.85 ("a dot where a cross belongs",
      the commonest conflict; e.g. putt fixture cam1, cross at 58 deg).
   c) A specular highlight on the central mark: the glint fixture
      (cam1 16.0908) - the glinted cross has solidity 0.65 and 26.6 mm2 and
      is clutter, so both cameras refuse ("only 2 classified marks"). The
      rendered-glint test of Task 3 does not carry over to this real glint.
   None of these makes a wrong pose; they make refusals.

4. Wrong poses
   No wrong identification was found: every sign is right, and no
   comparison is off by anything like a symmetry of the site set (72 / 120 /
   180 deg). Lone poses (one camera oriented, the other refused) carried
   into the other camera: 26 cases; in 4 a classified blob of the other
   camera lies off every site (putt 1 8.8708, 8.9194, putt 2 16.3316, putt 3
   44.8795). For 8.8708 the neighbouring rolling comparison is within 5 %
   and 15 deg, so that pose is right and the off-site blob is clutter that
   classified; 16.3316 is in the noisy end of putt 2 (see 2.).
   Precision: 4 of 15 rolling frames with both cameras oriented disagree by
   3.0-4.0 deg, above the static acceptance number; with flagged centres
   left out 1 of 7 (3.36 deg). See "Centre quality" in 2.

5. Open points of Task 4, checked here
   - Missing-ink check reads the raw dark mask: in no frame of the three
     putts did a site pass that check only on dark pixels the 2 mm opening
     removes (orientation_check.missing_ink_audit, 0 of 0). It did not
     matter on these frames.
   - Dim lower limb (Review Focus 2): limb fixture cam2 44.9315 gives an
     Orientation, 5 marks, rms 1.11 deg; no limb shading became a blob
     within 60 deg. cam1 refuses the same instant (central bar bitten into a
     "dot" by the glint, left cross blurred to a "dot"); cam2's pose carried
     into cam1 puts all 4 of cam1's classified blobs on predicted sites
     (2 matching, 2 of the misread shapes).
